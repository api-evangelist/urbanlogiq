# UrbanLogiq

> AI-powered data platform helping governments make better, faster decisions across transportation, planning, public safety, and economic development.

UrbanLogiq builds a secure data platform and a Global Foundation Model for the public sector. Our tools unify siloed government data so agencies can monitor networks, target investment, and improve community outcomes.

## Product

- [Data Platform](/data-platform): Unified data platform for government — integration, analytics, and decision support.
- [Global Foundation Model](/global-foundation-model): Cross-jurisdictional AI model trained on community behavior at scale.
- [Ethica](/ethica): Government-grade generative AI insights engine.
- [Trust Center](/trust-center): Security, privacy, and compliance posture.

## Solutions

- [Transportation](/solutions/transportation): Traffic safety, mobility analytics, and Vision Zero.
- [Planning](/solutions/planning): Land use, zoning, and long-range planning tools.
- [Public Safety](/solutions/public-safety): Fire risk, crime, and emergency response analytics.
- [Economic Development](/solutions/economic-development): Investment targeting and main-street insights.
